# Communication Quality

Strength: `Mandatory`

Scope: All communication.

## Shared Standard

- Preserve factual, logical, domain, and contractual accuracy.
- When they can change interpretation or execution, state material distinctions, relationships,
  predicates, required outcomes, qualifications, exceptions, ownership, completion conditions, and
  stop conditions explicitly enough to support one intended interpretation or action.
- Use consistent terminology. Prefer clarity to brevity; shorten content only when required meaning
  and boundaries remain intact.
- Communicate conclusions and material supporting evidence, assumptions, and uncertainty without
  exposing private chain-of-thought.

## Audience Routing

- In user-facing communication, use plain language appropriate to the user's technical understanding
  and briefly explain necessary technical terms without erasing distinctions that affect
  correctness.
- When creating or materially editing an Agent-consumed document intended to instruct or constrain
  an Agent's decisions or actions, apply `writing-for-agents`.
- For artifacts serving humans and Agents, apply user-facing guidance to presentation and
  `writing-for-agents` to operative content; a human-readable summary must not replace or weaken the
  operative contract.
