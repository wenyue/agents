# Communication Quality

Strength: `Mandatory`

Scope: Accuracy, clarity, ambiguity control, and audience adaptation across user-facing
communication and Agent-consumed artifacts.

## Shared Standard

- Preserve factual, logical, domain, and contractual accuracy in every expression.
- State material distinctions, relationships, conditions, qualifications, and exceptions explicitly
  enough to support one intended interpretation.
- Use terminology consistently. Prefer clarity to brevity, and shorten content only when its
  required meaning and boundaries remain intact.
- Communicate conclusions and material supporting evidence, assumptions, and uncertainty without
  exposing private chain-of-thought.

## User-Facing Communication

- Use plain language appropriate to the user's technical understanding. Briefly explain necessary
  technical terms without erasing distinctions that affect correctness.

## Agent-Consumed Artifacts

- Write Specs, Plans, documentation, Rules, Skills, and other Agent-consumed artifacts precisely,
  clearly, and concisely enough for another Agent to determine the required decision or action
  without inventing a condition, fact, owner, step, or exit.
- State applicable predicates, required outcomes, exceptions, ownership, completion conditions, and
  stop conditions when they can change execution. Use ordered steps only when order affects the
  result.

## Mixed Audiences

- Apply both audience branches when an artifact serves humans and Agents. A human-readable summary
  must not replace or weaken the operative contract.
